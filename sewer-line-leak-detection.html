Tools and methods

Tools and Methods We Use

01

RIDGID SeeSnake Camera

Pushes through the lateral to record the interior condition of the pipe and pinpoint cracks, offsets, and root entry points.

02

Sewerin Acoustic Detection Unit

Listens for the sound of escaping water through soil and concrete to confirm the leak location from the surface.

03

Fluke Thermal Imaging Camera

Detects temperature differences caused by leaking wastewater, useful when the line is deep or the surface is paved.

04

Tracer Gas Leak Detector

Introduces a safe tracer gas into the line and tracks where it escapes, confirming the break when camera views are blocked.

05

Pipe Locator and Moisture Meter

Maps the underground path of the lateral and measures surface moisture to separate sewer leaks from supply or irrigation issues.

06

Video Pipe Inspection

A camera run from the cleanout to the city main records the full lateral. We review the footage with you and flag every defect by distance from the cleanout.

07

Acoustic Leak Detection

We place sensors along the line and listen for the frequency of escaping water. This method works through soil, sod, and up to 4 inches of concrete.

08

Pressure Testing

We isolate the lateral and pressurize it to confirm the leak is active. The test also tells us how severe the loss is before we quote a repair.

09

Epoxy Pipe Liner Application

For corroded or root-damaged runs, we apply an epoxy pipe liner that cures inside the existing pipe. It restores the line without full excavation and meets IAPMO standards.

Sewer Problems We Solve in Redlands

Most sewer line calls in Redlands trace back to one of these four failures. We find which one is yours and fix it at the source.

01

Soggy patch in the yard that never dries

We run the camera and acoustic gear to find the break, then repair the section with new PVC pipe and proper bedding. Most yard breaks are point repairs, not full replacements. Wastewater is surfacing and feeding bacteria into your soil. Left alone, it undermines landscaping and can reach the foundation.

02

Every drain in the house is slow at once

We camera the main line, locate the obstruction or break, and clear or repair it. If roots are the cause, we cut them out and discuss epoxy pipe liner to stop the next intrusion. A single slow sink is a trap issue. Every drain backing up points to a blockage or collapse in the main lateral.

03

Sewage smell in the yard or near the cleanout

We pressure-test the lateral, confirm the leak with a tracer gas leak detector, and seal or replace the failed joint. The smell is gone once the line is tight. Gas is escaping through a cracked joint or a broken cleanout seal. It is a health issue and often a sign the line is already leaking.

04

Water bill jumped with no change in habits

We isolate the sewer from the supply side using a pressure testing kit and moisture meter. Once we know which system is losing water, we locate and repair the exact break. A hidden sewer or supply leak can waste hundreds of gallons a month and quietly damage the slab or soil.
