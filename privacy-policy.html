Privacy and data

Information We Collect

When you contact us, we collect the information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the leak or plumbing issue you need help with.

When you browse our website, we automatically collect basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages you visit, and the time of your visit. We use this to keep the site working and improve it.

How We Use Information

We use your contact details to schedule your leak detection appointment, send updates, and follow up after the work is done. If you call us, we use your number to reach you about your service.

We use website data to understand how people find and use our site, fix problems, and make sure our services reach the right homeowners in Redlands and nearby areas. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking tools to remember your preferences and measure site traffic. Cookies are small files stored on your device.

You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. If you turn off cookies, some parts of our website may not work as well.

Analytics and Third-Party Tools

We use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to collect anonymous data about website visits. These tools help us see which pages are popular and where we can improve.

These third-party providers have their own privacy policies. We do not control how they handle data, but we only work with providers that meet standard privacy requirements.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, such as scheduling software or payment processors, and only as needed to serve you.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer. In those cases, we share only what is necessary.

Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal and accounting requirements, and resolve any disputes. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

Website analytics data is kept for a limited time, usually no more than 26 months, unless a longer period is required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These steps include secure servers, encryption where appropriate, and limiting access to your data to employees who need it.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

User Rights

You have the right to ask us what personal information we hold about you, to correct it if it is wrong, and to request that we delete it. You can also ask us to stop using your information for marketing.

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date at the top.

We encourage you to review this page periodically so you stay informed about how we protect your information.
