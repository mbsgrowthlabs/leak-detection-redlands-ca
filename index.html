Problems we solve

Leak Problems We Fix Around Redlands

Redlands homes deal with hard water, older copper runs and tree roots that find every seam in a sewer line. Here are the calls we get most, and how we handle them without turning your house upside down.

01

Water bill doubled but nothing looks wet

That is a classic hidden leak, often under the slab or in the yard line. We pressure-test each zone, listen with acoustic gear and use a moisture meter to narrow it down. Most of these get found in under two hours without opening the floor.

02

Warm spot on the floor and a ticking sound

A warm patch over a slab usually means a hot-water line leak underneath. We confirm it with thermal imaging, map the run with a pipe locator, then reroute or repair the line. You keep the flooring in most cases.

03

Pool losing water faster than evaporation

More than two inches a week is a leak, not the sun. We pressure-test the pool plumbing, check the shell and light fittings, and use leak detection dye to find the breach. Most pool leaks trace back to a fitting or a cracked return line.

04

Sewer smell in the yard and a slow drain

That points to root intrusion or a cracked sewer joint. We run a video pipe inspection camera through the line, find the break, and give you the repair options. Left alone, these turn into a full backup inside the house.
